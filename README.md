Core Gameplay Mechanics
The Ricochet Rule ("Crimson-Hot" Rounds): Direct shots fired straight at enemies fizzle out and deal zero damage. Bullets must bounce off at least one wall or obstacle first to become energized into lethal, glowing Crimson-Hot rounds capable of destroying enemies.

Tactical Slide (C Key): Execute a momentum-preserving slide to duck beneath enemy lines of sight and reposition dynamically under fire.

Slow-Motion Focus (Bullet Time): Dilate time on demand to calculate complex multi-wall bank shots and navigate high-pressure firefights with surgical precision.

Aggressive Enemy AI: Enemies actively hunt and pursue you across dark cyberpunk arenas—standing still or hiding in one place is a death sentence.

Style Chains & Multipliers: Multi-wall bank shots reward you with bonus tokens and style multipliers, encouraging creative geometric problem-solving.

Economy & Weapon Store
Bounty Tokens: Earn tokens for every stylish ricochet kill.

Weapon Progression: Spend your earned tokens in the in-game store to unlock advanced rifles and gear with custom stats, enhanced velocity, and neon optics.

Instant Map Selection: Choose your preferred dark cyberpunk arena right from the main menu before jumping into battle.

Technical Architecture
Engine & Renderer: Three.js / WebGL for high-performance browser-based 3D graphics, post-processing bloom, and dynamic lighting.

Physics & Collision: Custom vector reflection math using surface normals for real-time bullet bouncing, wall interactions, and spark particle emissions.

Input Management: Robust pointer-lock API handling for seamless first-person mouse look, responsive WASD movement, and action bindings.
